Net protocol example

Direct check-in

Net control · AF0FR sends

CQ CQ GATEWAY NET DE AF0FR AF0FR QNI K

Checking-in station · N0ABC sends

N0ABC N0ABC 12 WPM

Net control · AF0FR replies at 12 WPM and sends the net question

N0ABC DE AF0FR GE 12 WPM OK
FAV ANT ES WHY? K

Checking-in station · N0ABC answers

AF0FR DE N0ABC DIPOLE ES SIMPLE K

Net control · AF0FR closes the contact and calls for more check-ins

TU 73
GATEWAY NET DE AF0FR QNI? K

Add N0ABC as a direct check-in when heard. Its status is QSO open; select Close QSO after the final acknowledgment.

Calling a pending online station

K0XYZ checks in on the page

K0XYZ appears in the list as Pending. No radio contact has occurred yet.

K0XYZ K0XYZ DE AF0FR AF0FR GATEWAY NET K

Online station · K0XYZ answers and gives preferred speed

AF0FR DE K0XYZ K0XYZ 12 WPM K

Net control · AF0FR matches speed and asks the net question

K0XYZ DE AF0FR GE 12 WPM OK
FAV ANT ES WHY? K

Online station · K0XYZ answers

AF0FR DE K0XYZ EFHW ES MULTIBAND K

Net control · AF0FR closes the contact and calls for more check-ins

TU 73
GATEWAY NET DE AF0FR QNI? K

After the final acknowledgment, select Close QSO for K0XYZ.

QSY to the next band

Net control · AF0FR announces the move

GATEWAY NET DE AF0FR
QSY 40M 7055 NOW AR

Mark 40 m LIVE SOON before the announcement. After moving, select QSY here / make live; this closes the previous band card.

Closing the net

Net control · AF0FR sends

GATEWAY NET DE AF0FR
QNF TU ALL 73 SK

QNF indicates the net is closed. After sending the close, select Close net on the page.

Operating notes

  • All speeds are welcome; send your preferred speed when checking in.
  • A direct check-in starts as QSO open. Close it after the question, answer, and final acknowledgment.
  • An online check-in remains Pending until NCS calls the station and completes the on-air contact.
  • Checking in a direct station again reopens its QSO without adding a duplicate list entry.
  • Relays using QSP and repeat check-ins on each band are encouraged; coordinate relay callbacks on the air.
  • Before each QSY, announce the next band and frequency. Making the next card live closes the previous band; the net does not return.
  • Close the on-air net with QNF, then close it on the page to preserve the completed record.
  • Copy the net question over the air before revealing it here when possible.
  • Callsigns, frequency reports, and check-ins are public and not identity-verified.
